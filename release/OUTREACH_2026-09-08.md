```text

Early alpha. Which post-run questions do you find yourself answering repeatedly?
https://github.com/codex-improvement-lab/agent-aftertrace
```

```text
Which loop hurts most: rereading code, rerunning tests, or adding prints? I'm building Aftertrace to query already-recorded Python values. No token-savings claim yet; I'd love a small public repro to test whether it helps:
```
